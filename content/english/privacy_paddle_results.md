                                                    <h4>THE SHORT VERSION</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    <strong>Paddle Results does not collect personal data.</strong> There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
                                                    There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no tracking and no analytics in the app. We do not build a profile
                                                    of you, and we have nothing to sell or share, because we never receive it.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    The app saves the following on your device only, so that it can remember your choices between sessions:
                                                    </p>
                                                    <ul style='font-size: 16px !important; line-height: 1.5 !important; padding-left: 40px !important; margin: 16px 0 !important; list-style: disc outside !important;'>
                                                    <li>Athletes, races and competitions you save to your favourites</li>
                                                    <li>Races you have selected for comparison, and any comparisons you have named and saved</li>
                                                    <li>Your recent searches</li>
                                                    <li>Your answer to the analytics consent prompt</li>
                                                    </ul>
                                                    <p>
                                                    This information stays in the app's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else,
                                                    it is not backed up to our servers, and we cannot read it. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also clear it
                                                    at any time from within the app by removing your saved items.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>CONNECTIONS THE APP MAKES</h4>
                                                    <p>To show you results, the app contacts the following services:</p>

                                                    <h5>1. The Paddle Results API (results.quko.es), operated by QUKO</h5>
                                                    <p>
                                                    The app requests race, athlete, competition and ranking data. These requests do not carry any account, identifier
                                                    or profile, because none exists. As with any internet service, our servers record standard technical connection
                                                    data — IP address, timestamp, and the resource requested — which we use to operate the service, keep it secure and
                                                    diagnose faults. We do not use this data to identify or profile individual users, and we do not combine it with
                                                    anything else.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h5>2. Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com), a third-party service</h5>
                                                    <p>
                                                    The app uses this only to work out the time zone of a race venue, so that race times display correctly. All that is
                                                    sent are the venue's coordinates or the name of the competition venue — a public sporting location, not you.
                                                    </p>
                                                    <p>
                                                    <strong>The app never requests, uses or transmits your device's location.</strong> It holds no location permission.
                                                    Venue coordinates come from our own archive data.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h5>3. Links you choose to open</h5>
                                                    <p>
                                                    If you tap a link to an external website, it opens in an in-app browser window. Those sites are governed by their
                                                    own privacy policies, not this one.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>PERMISSIONS</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    The app requests <strong>internet access only</strong>. It does not ask for, and cannot access, your camera,
                                                    microphone, photos, files, contacts, calendar, location or health data. It does not send push notifications.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>SHARING</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    When you share a race, athlete or page, the app hands a plain web link to your device's own share sheet. You choose
                                                    the destination. The link contains only what you were looking at and any filters you had applied. We are not told
                                                    that you shared anything, or with whom.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    The only processing we carry out is the technical connection data described above, on the basis of our legitimate
                                                    interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in delivering a working, secure service. Everything else stays on your device and is
                                                    not processed by us at all.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>RETENTION</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Server connection logs are kept only for
                                                    the limited period needed for security and diagnostics, and are then deleted.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS</h4>
                                                    <p>
                                                    Our API is served from within the European Union. Open-Meteo is operated from the European Union. Where any transfer
                                                    outside the European Economic Area occurs, it is carried out under the safeguards required by Chapter V of the GDPR.
                                                    </p>

                                                    <h4>CHILDREN</h4>

                                                    Paddle Results is a sports results archive suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal data from anyone,
                                                    including children, and contains no user accounts, messaging, user-generated content or advertising.
                                                    </p>
